# Chapter 6 Tutorial Map (Class 6)

Chapter 6 in PHPCrashCourse focuses on iteration (loops). It covers `while`
and `for` loops, introduces `do...while` as a variation of `while`, and
explains tools for controlling loop flow and avoiding runaway scripts.

## Planned Tutorial Sequence

1. `6.1` While loops (conditions, counters, flags)
2. `6.2` do...while loops (guaranteed one execution)
3. `6.3` break and continue (exiting vs skipping)
4. `6.4` for loops (fixed repetition, increment/decrement)
5. `6.5` Alternative loop syntax (template-friendly `endwhile`, `endfor`)
6. `6.6` Avoiding infinite loops (guardrails, debugging patterns)

## Notes

- Chapter 6 mentions `foreach`, but defers it until arrays are introduced in
  Chapter 7.
